# Prototype asset notes

All prototype art and audio are generated by tools/generate_assets.py.

- textures/afterlight_atlas.png: editable 512×512 source atlas with asphalt, facade/window, grass and concrete regions.
- textures/afterlight_atlas.ktx: genuine Khronos KTX 1.1 RGBA8 texture with 10 mip levels. It is not a renamed PNG. The initial asset uses a broadly compatible RGBA fallback; ASTC/ETC2 GPU compression is a later optimization.
- audio/engine_loop.wav: original synthesized engine loop.
- audio/dispatcher_first_light.wav: locally synthesized temporary dispatcher voice line, not final cast recording.
