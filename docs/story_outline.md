# Story outline

A small coastal city loses its communications network during a strange rolling power failure. The player is a local courier and volunteer driver asked to reconnect five routes before the storm front arrives. The story focuses on driving, exploration, rescue and the people waiting at the other end of each run.

1. **First Light**: reconnect three city signal relays. This is the first playable mission.
2. **Night Shift**: deliver replacement parts by motorcycle along the waterfront.
3. **Dead Signal**: take a utility vehicle up a mountain pass to help a stranded maintenance crew.
4. **Crosswind**: guide a small convoy through a storm corridor.
5. **Last Run**: follow the coast and climb into the valley at sunrise.

## First Light dispatcher line
"You copy? The city grid is flickering again. Hit the three signal gates, then take the south road out. Keep your speed steady. I'll talk you through it."
